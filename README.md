# Privacy Policy for Everyday Widget

**Effective date: 15 November 2026**

**App:** Everyday Widget  
**Android package:** com.cizmi.everydaywidget  
**Developer:** CIZMI  
**Privacy contact:** support@cizmi.com

Everyday Widget is a lightweight, standalone calendar, widget and personal notes app. This Privacy Policy explains what information the app accesses, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## 1. Our approach to privacy

- **No advertisements**
- **No advertising or analytics SDKs**
- **No in-app purchases or subscriptions**
- **No account or registration required**
- **Personal notes and events are stored on your device**
- **Core calendar, widget and note features work offline**

The app does not upload your personal notes, photos, videos, handwriting or calendar entries to CIZMI or to its historical-content providers. We do not operate an app server that collects this personal content.

Online extras contact external services to retrieve historical stories, pictures and an approximate country. These connections involve limited technical information, as explained below.

## 2. Information stored on your device

Everyday Widget stores information locally to provide the features you choose to use. This may include:

- Notes, personal events, birthdays and anniversaries
- Photos, videos and handwriting added to notes
- Reminder schedules and notification preferences
- Widget layouts, backgrounds, fonts, colors and saved styles
- Language, country and weekend preferences
- Downloaded historical stories, images and source credits

This information is used to display your widgets and calendar, organize your personal entries, deliver reminders and remember your preferences.

If you allow access to your device calendar, the app can read and display entries from selected calendars. This access is optional and read-only. Everyday Widget does not modify those calendars or upload their contents. Any synchronization performed by your existing calendar provider is separate from Everyday Widget.

## 3. Online extras and external services

**Online extras are enabled by default. You can turn them off in Settings.** Core offline features remain available when online extras are disabled.

### Historical stories and pictures

The app contacts Wikipedia, Wikimedia Commons and their image hosts to retrieve historical information, pictures and licensing details.

These services receive your network IP address, a basic app identifier called a user-agent, and the information needed to answer a request, such as a language, calendar date, article title or image reference.

Your personal notes, attached photos and videos, handwriting, birthdays and private calendar entries are not included in these requests.

These services operate under their own privacy practices:

[Wikimedia Foundation Privacy Policy](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy)

### Automatic country selection

When country selection is set to Auto, and both online extras and automatic country detection are enabled, the app may contact **country.is** to determine your country from your network IP address.

The result helps select relevant holidays, weekend defaults and historical content. The app stores the returned country code and a refresh timestamp locally; it does not store the returned IP address. Country detection is attempted at most once every seven days.

The app does not request GPS or precise-location access. An IP-based country estimate may be inaccurate, especially when using a VPN.

You can disable automatic country detection or select a country manually.

Service information: [country.is](https://country.is/)

### Your controls

Turning online extras off stops new requests for online content. Requests already in progress may finish, and previously downloaded content may remain visible.

External providers are responsible for their own handling and retention of information they receive. Opening a source link launches your browser, where the destination website’s privacy practices apply.

## 4. Permissions and device access

Depending on the features you use, Everyday Widget may use:

- **Internet and network access:** to retrieve online extras
- **Read calendar:** to display selected device-calendar entries, if you grant permission
- **Notifications:** to deliver reminders you enable
- **Precise alarm access:** optionally, to improve reminder timing
- **Restart detection:** to restore scheduled reminders and widget updates after your device restarts

Photos and videos are selected through Android’s document picker
